# Case Study: Inventory Analysis using ABC & Pareto Frameworks

[📊 Download Raw Excel Dataset](https://lynn-khant-minn.github.io/Portfolio/abc_pareto_analysis.xls)

## 📌 1. The Bottleneck (Problem)
Unclassified warehouse inventory created tracking inefficiencies, holding up working capital in low-velocity stock.

## 🛠️ 2. Methodology & Tools
* **Optimization Framework:** ABC Categorization, Pareto (80/20 Rule) Distribution
* **Data Tools:** Advanced Excel (Power Query, Dynamic Array Formulas)

## 📊 3. Core Analysis & Findings
* Evaluated SKU distributions to classify inventory into A, B, and C priority tiers.
* Modeled cumulative consumption values to isolate top-tier financial drivers.

## 📈 4. Quantifiable Business Impact
* **Isolated the top 20% of high-value SKUs (Category A)** driving ~80% of total annual inventory carrying costs.
